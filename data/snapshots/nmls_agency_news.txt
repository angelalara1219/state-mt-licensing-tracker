Massachusetts Division of Banks issues a Regulatory Notice regarding Money Transmitter Licensing Requirements
Posted 8/5/2026 2:07 PM
July 31, 2026: Regulatory Notice
: Reminder of Money Transmitter Licensing Requirements under MGL c. 169B
The Division of Banks began accepting money transmitter license applications through NMLS on July 1, 2025. All entities engaged in money transmission activities under Massachusetts General Laws chapter 169B were required to submit an application for a money transmitter license through the NMLS by July 1, 2026, unless the entity was licensed as a Foreign Transmittal Agency and/or a Check Seller with an approved license as of Dec. 31, 2025. Those entities transitioned to the new money transmitter license by filing a transition request through the NMLS during the NMLS renewal period starting Nov. 1, 2025. ​
As a reminder, any person or entity that is required to obtain a money transmitter license under MGL c. 169B and did not submit an application on or before July 1, 2026,
must immediately cease
all licensable activity and is prohibited from engaging in money transmission activity in Massachusetts unless and until the required license has been granted.
Failure to obtain any required license under MGL c. 169B, could subject any person or entity to penalties, including the imposition of civil penalties under MGL c. 169B, §10.

Nevada Division of Mortgage Lending Enacts Cyber Security Program and Breach Reporting Requirements
Posted 3/30/2026 3:21 PM
